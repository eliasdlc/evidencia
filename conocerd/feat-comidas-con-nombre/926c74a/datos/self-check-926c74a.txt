How the route screen labels a meal stop and a dinner (code, itinerary_day_list.dart)
- `_mealLine(SuggestedStop)` at :1119 to 1135: lunch with a place reads S.mealLunchHere ("Almuerza aquí"); lunch with no place and a zone reads S.mealLunchLooseIn(zone) ("Almuerza por <zona>"); with no zone S.mealLunchLoose. Dinner with a place reads S.mealDinnerHere ("Cena aquí"); with no place and a zone S.mealDinnerLooseIn(zone) ("Cena por <zona>"); with no zone S.mealDinnerLoose ("Cena por la zona"). Snack reads S.mealSnackHere ("Merienda aquí"). The strings are at strings.dart:2448 to 2455 and :3377 to 3394. The same switch is repeated in onboarding/presentation/widgets/hilo/paso_ruta.dart:323 to 335, so the wizard preview and the route screen agree.

That is Maraca and Buche Perico in the frame. A meal with no place is its own card (`_TarjetaComida`, :1137 onward) with a restaurant icon, the `_mealLine` as title and "<hora> . Sugerida" under it; its hour comes from `looseMealStarts` (:360 to 397), where a dinner takes the planner's hour when that is later than the end of the previous stop (:396).
- The dinner hour of a dinner with a place: `mealNotBefore(day.meals)` (meal_planner.dart:208 to 211, only `hasPlace && kind == dinner`) is passed as `notBefore` to `arrivalMinutes` (:660), so the card waits for the dinner hour. A 7:00 PM arrival that is not tied to the previous stop is therefore the clock's wait and not a stale value.
- `DayClosing` for the night row: `kind: night != null ? DayClosingKind.night : ...` (:244 to 252) with `isLastDay ? null : nights[dayNumber]` (:232), so day 1 of a 2-day circuit gets the night row and "Noche 1 de 1" is `nightIndex` 1 of `nightTotal` 1. Matches the frame.
